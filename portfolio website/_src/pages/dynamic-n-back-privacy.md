# Dynamic N-Back privacy policy

**Effective October 26, 2026.** This policy covers the Dynamic N-Back Android app (`com.evolved.automata.app.dynamicnback`), published on Google Play by **Andrew Baughns**, the developer. Questions go to [abaughns12@gmail.com](mailto:abaughns12@gmail.com).

## Summary

- Dynamic N-Back has **no user accounts**.
- Your player profiles, game settings, scores, history and themes are **stored only on your device**.
- The app sends the developer **anonymous usage and crash data** through Google Firebase.
- The app has no ads, and your data is **never sold**.

## Data stored on your device

Player profiles, difficulty and timing settings, scores, session history and custom themes are kept in the app's private storage. Other apps cannot read it. The data is removed when you clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app.

## Data the developer receives

### Usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

Firebase records standard app events, such as app opens and screens viewed. It adds an app-instance identifier, device model, Android version, app version, and approximate country or region derived from your IP address. The developer uses this only to understand how the app is used. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Firebase privacy and security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

### Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app crashes, Crashlytics sends a report with the stack trace, installation identifiers, the time of the crash, app version, Android version, device model, and memory and disk details. Crashlytics keeps this data for 90 days, according to [Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

## Permissions

The app uses internet access only to send usage and crash reports. It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

## Keeping and deleting your data

There is no account to delete. Clear the app's storage in **Android Settings → Apps → Dynamic N-Back → Storage → Clear storage**, or uninstall the app. To ask for deletion of analytics or crash data tied to your device, email [abaughns12@gmail.com](mailto:abaughns12@gmail.com).

## Children

Dynamic N-Back is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
