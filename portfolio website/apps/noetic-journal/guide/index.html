Welcome to Noetic Journal

Noetic Journal is a programmable note tree: hierarchical notes and collections you already understand, plus a layer you control — purpose text on each note, Lisp macros, scheduled commands, and optional document managers that speak for a note when you use AI.

This is not “another chat app with agents.” The center of gravity is your writing and your rules. AI is optional (remote BYOK, local on-device, or none). You choose what is stored, what runs, and which providers you trust. Think of the app as a complementary cognitive device—technology that extends memory and planning without replacing your judgment.

What you can do day to day

Library and workspaces. Your Library tree holds collections (journal, tasks, reference, flashcards, and anything you add). Workspaces group notes for a project or context—see workspaces.

Write and organize. Every note has a title for search and links. Parent/child structure keeps diaries, projects, and “my things” lists tidy. The note Tools sheet adds photos, camera captures, speech handlers, presets, and sync actions. About → Storage can report on-disk size (note file + sidecar) when you tap Calculate on-disk size.

Talk with your notes (optional). Open Talk to me on a note for simple chat by default (ask, tell, command). Turn on context chat or Hi-IQ in the discourse dialog when you need executable Lisp in the loop or broader tree context—power-user modes, not the default pitch. Attach images and enable Include images in discourse so vision-capable models can see linked photos.

Help, not menus. From the menu, open Help and describe what you want (“remind me to …”, “download and play …”, “how do I filter journal entries?”). The app finds instructions and can Show me a demo when one exists.

Search at scale. Menu → Search streams partial results while a job runs—handy when you cannot remember which collection held a note. See search your notes.

AI is optional. Use remote APIs, a private server, local on-device models (some builds support vision), or no AI at all. Verify important answers—generators can be wrong.

Look and feel. Global themes (Cyber, Dark, Rose, Sepia, and others) apply across the app; individual notes can override accent colors for editing. About (under the Tools sheet) lists version info and legal notices.
