Search your notes

Menu → Search runs keyword jobs across large libraries. Results stream in as partial matches so you can preview hits before the job finishes—useful when you have thousands of notes in Noetic_Journal, me, and knowledgebase.

Start a search

  1. Open Search from the menu.
  2. Type your query (use “quotes” for an exact phrase).
  3. Choose keyword vs semantic modes and document vs collection scope as needed.
  4. Under Collections to search, check the trees you want (or Select all).
  5. Raise Max partial results if you expect many hits (default 20).
  6. Tap Start search.

While it runs

The selected query shows progress; Partial results fill in as documents match. Use Pause, Cancel, or Accept all when you are satisfied.

Open a hit

Each card shows title, path, and hits: keywords that matched. Preview opens a quick read-only view; Open jumps to the note; Accept keeps the hit in your result set.

Tips

  • Limit by date when you remember roughly when you edited the note.
  • Search title and body together for most everyday lookups.
  • For “how do I …?” questions, Help is often faster than Search.
