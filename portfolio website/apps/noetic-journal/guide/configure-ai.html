Cloud AI (remote models)

Remote generative AI powers Talk to me on notes, semantic Search, natural-language Help, and many automations. You bring your own API keys (BYOK) or point the app at a private server endpoint your build supports.

What to configure

  1. Open Profile → Model configuration from the menu (or Model configuration).
  2. Under Ready or Available, enable a remote provider (OpenAI-compatible, Gemini, Anthropic, and others depending on your build).
  3. Assign HIGH and LOW roles when prompted—HIGH for quality chat, LOW for quick background tasks.
  4. For notes with photos, pick a model that supports vision when you want the assistant to see attached images.

Privacy and terms

Traffic goes to the provider you choose under their terms and privacy policy. API keys stay on your device unless you export your journal. For sensitive material, prefer Configure local AI or skip remote models until you need them (see the welcome tour optional setup links).

Try it

  • Open a note → Talk to me (chat) to discuss that document’s scope.
  • Menu → Help → ask how do I chat with this document?
  • Attach images on a note and enable Include images in discourse chat when the model supports vision.
