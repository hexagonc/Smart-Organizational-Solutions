Speech and voice

Noetic Journal can listen for commands and speak answers. Use cloud speech services or on-device recognition, depending on your build and feature choices.

Enable speech

  1. Use the first-run onboarding checkboxes, or open Profile → Speech recognition after setup.
  2. Turn on speech recognition (and download the on-device model if offered).
  3. Grant Microphone permission when Android prompts you.

Wake phrases and handlers

Background listening uses speech handlers—Lisp scripts matched to phrases you define. Configure them from Profile → Speech handlers; beginners can rely on Help and the global wake phrase map in settings.

Voice output

Text-to-speech uses the system voice or your chosen engine under Profile → TTS voices. Scheduled reminders and chat replies can speak aloud when the action requests it.

Example

After setup, open Help and try “remind me to stretch at 3pm” or “add buy milk to my to-do list.”
